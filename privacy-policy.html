Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Chuppys Discord application (“Chuppys,” “the App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use or authorize the App.

1. Information We May Receive

Depending on the features you use, Chuppys may receive information made available through Discord authorization or the Discord API, such as your Discord user ID, username/display information, server (guild) information, role-connection information, and authorization information needed to provide the requested feature.

2. How We Use Information

We use information only as reasonably necessary to operate Chuppys, provide verification or role-connection features, maintain security, troubleshoot problems, and comply with applicable requirements.

3. We Do Not Sell Personal Information

We do not sell users’ personal information. We do not use Discord information for unrelated advertising or profiling.

4. Sharing

We may disclose information when necessary to operate the App, when required by law, or when needed to protect the security and integrity of Chuppys, Discord servers, users, or others. We do not intentionally disclose Discord API information for unrelated purposes.

5. Retention and Deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the feature that uses it, security, troubleshooting, or legal obligations. Where applicable, you may request deletion of information held by Chuppys by contacting the Chuppys server/app owner through the official Chuppys Discord community.

6. Discord

Chuppys uses Discord services and APIs. Discord separately processes information under Discord’s own policies. This Privacy Policy does not replace Discord’s policies.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information used by Chuppys. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Chuppys is not intended to circumvent Discord’s age requirements or applicable law. Users must meet Discord’s minimum age requirements and any other age requirements applicable where they live.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or its data practices change. The updated version will be posted here with a new “Last updated” date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the Chuppys server/app owner through the official Chuppys Discord community.
